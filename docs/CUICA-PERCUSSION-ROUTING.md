# Cuíca & Brazilian Percussion Electroacoustic Routing Guide

This guide details the acoustic physics, extreme transient management, membrane resonance control, and DSP preset configuration for **Electroacoustic Cuíca** and **Brazilian Percussion** (Pandeiro, Timbal, and Repinique) within UltraRig for M-VAVE hardware (Tank-G v1/v2, Tank-B, and Black Box).

## 1. Acoustic Profile & Dynamic Challenges of Membrane & Friction Percussion

Brazilian friction and hand percussion instruments exhibit some of the widest dynamic crest factors and sharpest transient spikes in live sound reinforcement (frequently exceeding $+20\text{ dB}$ peak-to-RMS ratios).

```
Cuíca (Friction Drum) / Pandeiro (Tambourine) / Timbal
   │
   ├──► [ Internal Clip-On Condenser Mic / Contact Piezo ]
   │
   ▼
Extreme Dynamic Challenges:
 1. Friction Squeak & Slip-Stick Energy (Cuíca bamboo stick + wet cloth: 700 Hz - 2.8 kHz)
 2. Explosive Slap Transients (Pandeiro / Timbal rim shots & slap strokes: 2.0 kHz - 6.5 kHz)
 3. Uncontrolled Membrane Boom (Loose goat/synthetic drumhead resonance: 90 Hz - 160 Hz)
 4. Stage Bleed & Acoustic Feedback (Live samba circles & carnival percussion rigs)
```

### 1.1 Instrument-Specific Acoustic Profiles

- **Cuíca (Brazilian Friction Drum):**
  - Sound is generated by rubbing a damp cloth along a thin bamboo stick (*gambiarra*) attached to the center of a tensioned goatskin drumhead.
  - Pitch is modulated continuously over ~1.5 octaves by pressing the skin near the stick's attachment point.
  - Produces a vocal, barking harmonic spectrum centered between $250\text{ Hz}$ and $2.4\text{ kHz}$, accompanied by high-frequency cloth-friction hiss ($4\text{ kHz} - 8\text{ kHz}$).
- **Pandeiro (Brazilian Frame Drum with Platinelas):**
  - Combines deep thumb strokes (*grave*, $70\text{ Hz} - 120\text{ Hz}$), crisp open slaps (*tapa*, $1.5\text{ kHz} - 3.5\text{ kHz}$), and metallic brass jingle shimmer (*platinelas*, $6\text{ kHz} - 14\text{ kHz}$).
- **Timbal (Bahian Conical Hand Drum):**
  - High-tension synthetic head tuned very high for piercing, cutting slaps (*bacalhau* and open tones) in outdoor trio elétrico and axé percussion sections.

## 4. Factory Scene Presets

### Scene A: Cuíca Melódica de Samba (Vocal Bark & Friction Clarity)
- **Concept:** Captures the expressive human-like pitch inflections of the cuíca while removing damp-cloth scraping hiss and stage rumble.
- **Filtering:** `HPF = 135 Hz`, resonant vocal push at `1.1 kHz (+3 dB)`, dynamic friction tamer at `4.5 kHz`.
- **Dynamics:** Gate at `-34 dBFS`, optical compressor `3.5:1` (`12 ms` attack, `110 ms` release).
- **Space:** Warm Wooden Samba Studio (`1.3 s`, `18%` mix).

### Scene B: Pandeiro Moderno Punch & Slap (Marcos Suzano Style)
- **Concept:** Balances cavernous low thumb tones (*grave*) against crisp slaps (*tapa*) and shimmering jingles (*platinelas*)—even for modern electronic-hybrid pandeiro setups.
- **Filtering:** `HPF = 50 Hz`, subtle sub-boost `+2.5 dB @ 75 Hz`, mid scoop `-3.5 dB @ 380 Hz` for clarity, air shelf `+2.0 dB @ 10 kHz`.
- **Dynamics:** Dual-stage peak limiter + `4:1` compressor bringing subtle finger rolls forward.
- **Space:** Dry Tight Room (`0.8 s`, `10%` mix) keeping syncopated rhythms razor sharp.

### Scene C: Timbal de Rua High-Energy (Outdoor Axé / Carnival)
- **Concept:** Maximum outdoor projection and snap for high-tension Bahian timbal without ear-fatiguing upper-mid harshness.
- **Dynamics:** Ultra-fast limiter (`0.2 ms` attack) clamping hard rim shots; heavy noise gate (`-28 dBFS`) to block crowd and brass bleed.
- **EQ:** Cut boxiness at `240 Hz (-5 dB)`, punchy attack boost at `2.6 kHz`, smooth roll-off above `8 kHz`.
- **Space:** Zero reverb (dry FOH punch) or subtle slapback.

### Scene D: Percussão Acústica Espacial Estéreo (Soundtrack & Ambient)
- **Concept:** Cinematic percussion processing using ping-pong stereo echoes and lush chamber reverb for atmospheric breaks and solo percussion intros.
- **Modulation & Delay:** Tempo-synced Dotted-8th Ping-Pong Delay (`375 ms`, `28%` feedback) with high-cut at `3.5 kHz`.
- **Space:** Large Stone Hall Reverb (`3.2 s`, `30%` mix).

## 5. Hardware Setup & Mapping

### Tank-B Control Assignments
- Tank-B's dedicated low-frequency headroom and aggressive high-pass/limiter stage make it an outstanding belt-pack or floor processor for pandeiro players using internal dual pickups (Marcos Suzano method).
- **Footswitch 1:** Scene A (Cuíca) / Scene B (Pandeiro Punch).
- **Footswitch 2:** Scene D (Ambient Echo Swell for Percussion Solos).

### Tank-G (v1 / v2) Control Assignments
- Disable amp/cab modeling blocks (set Amp Bypass / Flat Preamp) and utilize the Noise Gate, Parametric EQ, Compressor, and Delay/Reverb matrix.
- **Knob 1 (GATE):** Bleed rejection threshold for loud stages.
- **Knob 2 (COMP):** Peak limiting and finger-roll sensitivity.
- **Knob 3 (REVERB):** Room ambience blend.

### Black Box Desktop Configuration
- **Mic Preamp / Line Pad:** Engage input attenuation if using high-output active internal drum microphones to avoid clipping the analog front-end prior to DSP conversion.
- **Balanced Direct Out:** Provides noise-free FOH connectivity without requiring external DI boxes on stage.
